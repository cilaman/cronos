`Worker._publish` is `async def` but `WorkerProtocol._publish` is sync — passing `Worker` directly as `event_worker` silently drops events as un-awaited coroutines. `_WorkerProtocolAdapter._publish` must be a sync bridge writing to both `_run_buffer` (replay) and `_subscribers[task_id]` (live SSE).
