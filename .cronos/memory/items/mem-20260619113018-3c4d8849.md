WorkerPool in `backend/app/worker_pool.py` now accepts `harness_store: HarnessStore | None = None` in `__init__` and passes it through to `Worker` in `start_for_space()`; `main.py` at line ~400 passes `harness_store=harness_store` to the WorkerPool constructor — this was the root cause of the harness executor never running (worker.harness_store was always None).
