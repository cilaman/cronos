Pipeline gate for `implementation` phase requires `iteration_id` (e.g. `I3`) and `metrics` mapping in the impl-report YAML header, plus required sections `## Files changed`, `## Out-of-scope findings`, `## Assumptions`, `## Open questions`, `## Next consumer brief`. Without these the verifier exits 1. Also the impl-report must be dual-written to `.cronos/pipeline/<goal_slug>/` (canonical verifier path) and `.cronos/pipeline/<parent-pipeline-dir>/` to match the scout/design artifacts.
